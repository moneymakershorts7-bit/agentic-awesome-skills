# Creative Resources Directory & Catalog

## Audio & Music Engines
- **Freesound.org**: Massive collaborative database of Creative Commons audio snippets, foley, and atmospheric field recordings.
- **Sonniss GDC Archive**: Multi-gigabyte professional SFX libraries released annually under permissive licensing.
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)**: Standard CC-BY library for background music across all genres.

## 3D Environments & PBR Materials
- **Poly Haven**: 100% CC0 public domain 8K HDRIs, photorealistic PBR materials, and game-ready 3D models.
- **ambientCG**: CC0 procedural and photogrammetry materials with normal, displacement, roughness, and AO maps.

## Video Pre-Production & Planning
- Storyboard layouts (16:9 widescreen and 9:16 vertical reels).
- Shooting schedule templates, call sheets, and gear checkout sheets.
- Talent release forms and location agreement templates.
