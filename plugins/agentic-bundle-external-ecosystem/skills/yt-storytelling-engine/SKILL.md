# YouTube Storytelling Engine (`yt-storytelling-engine`)

A deterministic, high-retention narrative scriptwriting framework engineered specifically for faceless YouTube documentaries, investigative essays, case studies, and explainer videos.

---

## 1. Core Mental Models

1. **The Viewer as Co-Protagonist**: The voiceover serves as an authoritative guide (Sherpa) leading the viewer through an epistemological transformation. The story is about the viewer's evolving worldview.
2. **The Expectation Gap (Robert McKee)**: Narrative events are not passive data points. A subject takes an action expecting outcome $A$; antagonistic reality counters with outcome $B$, ripping open "The Gap" that demands cognitive resolution.
3. **The Neural Glitch & Sacred Flaw (Will Storr)**: A compelling story is the psychological autopsy of a flawed "Theory of Control" ("If I do $X$, I will be invincible"). The collapse of this theory fuels dramatic tension.
4. **Relentless Value Polarity Shifts ($\Delta V \neq 0$)**: Every 45–90 second segment must alter the thematic charge from positive to negative ($+ \to -$) or negative to positive ($- \to +$). Zero-polarity segments are cut.
5. **The Open-Loop Relay**: Never resolve a narrative tension without immediately planting a larger, more perilous question before the sentence ends.

---

## 2. The 8-Step YouTube Story Circle (Dan Harmon & Campbell Adaptation)

For standard 10–14 minute videos (1,400–2,100 words at 145 WPM):

```
                     [1. YOU / The Baseline Comfort]
                (8. CHANGE)                 (2. NEED)
                         \                    /
            ORDER         \                  /         ORDER
            ------------------------------------------------
            CHAOS         /                  \         CHAOS
                         /                    \
                 (7. RETURN)                (3. GO)
                 (6. TAKE)                    (4. SEARCH)
                      [5. FIND / The False Peak]
```

1. **YOU (0:00–0:30, 0–4%)**: Establish the recognizable status quo. Invert expectations with a high-contrast pattern interrupt.
2. **NEED (0:30–1:30, 4–12%)**: Expose the existential flaw, anomaly, or impending systemic collapse.
3. **GO (1:30–3:00, 12–25%)**: Cross the threshold into the unfamiliar world. Commitment to the dangerous investigation.
4. **SEARCH (3:00–6:00, 25–50%)**: The Road of Trials. Rapid delivery of evidence, mini-battles, and escalating friction.
5. **FIND (6:00–7:30, 50–62%)**: The Midpoint Breakthrough. Discovery of the smoking gun or attainment of a deceptive False Victory.
6. **TAKE (7:30–9:30, 62–80%)**: The Heavy Price. Systemic retaliation. "All Is Lost" moment and the "Whiff of Death" (Snyder).
7. **RETURN (9:30–11:00, 80–92%)**: The Irreversible Climax. Final confrontation between the core antagonistic force and the truth.
8. **CHANGE (11:00–12:00, 92–100%)**: The Paradigm Shift. How the viewer's perception of reality is permanently rewired.

---

## 3. Sub-15-Second Hook Architecture ("The Cold Plunge")

Zero channel intros. Zero "Hey guys, welcome back."

* **0:00–0:03 (Pattern Interrupt)**: A violent sensory or intellectual contradiction of baseline expectations.
* **0:04–0:08 (Stake Injection)**: Why the viewer's money, safety, or belief system is directly threatened.
* **0:09–0:15 (Central Dramatic Question)**: The paradoxical riddle that can only be solved by watching until the end.

---

## 4. Scripting Archetypes

* **Documentary / Corporate Deep Dive**: Unraveling conspiracy arc (McKee Antagonism + Harmon Circle).
* **Biographical Rise & Fall**: The Icarus Paradigm (Storr Theory of Control + Snyder Beat Sheet).
* **Technical / System Explainer**: The Paradigm Shatter (Campbell Hero's Journey + Socratic Dissonance).
* **True Crime / Forensic Mystery**: The Labyrinth of Hypotheses (Loewenstein Gap + Snyder Bad Guys Close In).

---

## 5. Pre-Flight Script Quality Gate (DO-CONFIRM)

- [ ] Line 1 interrupts expectation in <3 seconds.
- [ ] Central Dramatic Question explicit before 0:15.
- [ ] Every 60–90 second block exhibits a net value shift ($\Delta V \neq 0$).
- [ ] At least 3 major expectation gaps rip open across the timeline.
- [ ] No minor question is answered without seeding a subsequent mystery.
- [ ] Climax stems directly from a sacrifice made in the Dark Night of the Soul.
