# Style Archetypes Reference Guide

Every video and channel must be anchored to a single visual archetype. Mixing archetypes creates cognitive dissonance, visual chaos, and an amateurish aesthetic.

---

## 1. Documentary Noir / Investigative Forensic

* **Genre Fit**: Geopolitics, corporate corruption, financial fraud, cold cases, covert military history.
* **Benchmark Creators**: *Lemmino*, *MagnatesMedia*, *Fern*, *Johnny Harris (investigative series)*.
* **Atmosphere**: Quiet tension, investigative weight, archival secrecy, tactile intelligence dossier.

### Visual & Grading Tokens
* **Base 60% Ground**: Obsidian Black (`#0A0D14`) to Deep Slate Grey (`#12151E`).
* **Secondary 30% Structure**: Matte Charcoal (`#1E222B`) and Warm Desaturated Steel (`#2B303C`).
* **Accent 10% Pop**: Archive Amber (`#D4AF37`) or Desaturated Crimson (`#991B1B`).
* **Film Stock / LUT**: Kodak Vision3 500T 5219 emulation, 35mm fine grain (3–5%), subtle halation on highlights.
* **Lighting**: Low-key directional tungsten, sharp chiaroscuro shadows, high micro-contrast, minimal ambient fill.

### Typography Stack
* **Headline / Hero Title**: *Fraunces* (Optical Size 72, Weight 700) or *Cormorant Garamond* (Bold, tracking -0.02em).
* **Metadata Stamp / Coordinates**: *JetBrains Mono* or *Space Mono* (Uppercase, tracking +0.12em, 11px/14px).
* **Body / Narration Subtitles**: *Inter* (Medium, 16px, clean white `#FAF7F2` with 2px semi-transparent black stroke).

### Motion & B-Roll Cadence
* Smooth, deliberate slow linear pushes (1.05x zoom over 8s).
* Archival document pans with physical paper texture overlay (creases, yellowed edges, stamps).
* High-resolution maps with subtle topographic contour lines, forensic crosshairs, and pinpoint coordinates.

## 2. Swiss Editorial / Data Journalism

* **Genre Fit**: Science explainers, economics, cultural critiques, policy teardowns, data deep dives.
* **Benchmark Creators**: *Vox*, *Johnny Harris*, *Cleo Abram*, *The Verge*.
* **Atmosphere**: Intellectual clarity, rigorous precision, journalistic integrity, tactile editorial print.

### Visual & Grading Tokens
* **Base 60% Ground**: Warm Newsprint Cream (`#F7F5F0`) or Dark Slate Paper (`#181A1F`).
* **Secondary 30% Structure**: Ink Black (`#121316`) or Off-Black Charcoal (`#22252A`).
* **Accent 10% Pop**: Safety Highway Yellow (`#FACC15`), Klein Blue (`#0047AB`), or Signal Vermillion (`#E11D48`).
* **Lighting / Grade**: High-key soft studio illumination, uniform white balance (5400K), zero vignette, crisp edge contrast.

### Typography Stack
* **Headline / Hero Title**: *Neue Haas Grotesk* (Display 75 Bold) or *Inter Tight* (Black, tracking -0.03em).
* **Secondary / Deck**: *Merriweather* (Regular/Italic, high legibility) or *Source Serif 4*.
* **Data Callouts & Labels**: *Aktiv Grotesk* or *Space Grotesk* (Medium, tracking +0.02em).

### Motion & B-Roll Cadence
* Crisp modular grid snapping (12-column Swiss grid).
* Animated data charts following Tufte's principles (direct data labeling, no legends, zero 3D extrusion).
* Kinetic paper cutouts, highlighter wipe animations, physical ruler and grease pencil overlays.

## 3. Technical Systems Cyber / Minimalist Hacker

* **Genre Fit**: Software engineering, cybersecurity, systems architecture, reverse engineering, tech hardware.
* **Benchmark Creators**: *Low Level Learning*, *Fireship*, *Ben Eater*, *ThePrimeagen*.
* **Atmosphere**: Terminal precision, deep focus, engineer’s workbench, zero corporate fluff.

### Visual & Grading Tokens
* **Base 60% Ground**: Terminal Void (`#0B1120`) to Dark Graphite (`#0F172A`).
* **Secondary 30% Structure**: Gunmetal Grey (`#334155`) and Deep Blue-Grey (`#1E293B`).
* **Accent 10% Pop**: Terminal Emerald (`#10B981`) or Laser Cyan (`#06B6D4`).
* **Lighting / Grade**: Cool ambient tone (6500K), deep blacks, crisp line contrast, subtle CRT scanline option (10% opacity).

### Typography Stack
* **Headline / Hero Title**: *JetBrains Mono* (ExtraBold, tracking -0.01em) or *Fira Code* (Bold).
* **Body & Code Blocks**: *Fira Code* or *IBM Plex Mono* (Regular, semantic syntax highlighting).
* **UI Labels & Tags**: *Inter* or *Geist* (Medium, 12px, rounded pill badges).

### Motion & B-Roll Cadence
* Fast, surgical pattern interrupts (every 2.5–4s).
* Direct AST/data-flow visualizers and hardware schematics.
* Code diff highlights with smooth cursor sweeps and mechanical typing SFX.

## 4. Cinematic Essay / Filmic Philosophy

* **Genre Fit**: Philosophy, human psychology, deep history, existential essays, biographical reflections.
* **Benchmark Creators**: *Aperture*, *Pursuit of Wonder*, *Solaris*, *Kinsella*.
* **Atmosphere**: Contemplative, poetic, profound, melancholic beauty, museum-grade cinematography.

### Visual & Grading Tokens
* **Base 60% Ground**: Filmic Charcoal (`#121316`) or Matte Night (`#16181D`).
* **Secondary 30% Structure**: Warm Tungsten Taupe (`#78716C`) and Muted Muted Earth (`#57534E`).
* **Accent 10% Pop**: Alabaster Cream (`#FAF7F2`) or Muted Ochre (`#D97706`).
* **Film Stock / LUT**: Kodak 2383 Print Film Emulation, anamorphic oval bokeh, 16mm/35mm grain blend.
* **Lighting**: Deep naturalistic shadows, golden hour raking side-light, atmospheric haze / dust motes.

### Typography Stack
* **Headline / Hero Title**: *Cinzel* (SemiBold, wide tracking +0.08em) or *Canela* / *Equestria*.
* **Secondary / Body**: *EB Garamond* (Regular/Italic, tracking 0.00em) or *Cormorant Infant*.
* **Subtitles**: Minimalist centered serif with soft gradient feathering.

### Motion & B-Roll Cadence
* Extended slow-burn tracking shots (6–10s holds).
* Parallax depth separation between foreground philosophical subject and distant horizon.
* Classical soundscapes and ambient field recordings (rain, wind, analog hum).

## 5. Minimalist Vector Explainer

* **Genre Fit**: Broad educational explainers, economics, simplified complex science, cognitive principles.
* **Benchmark Creators**: *Kurzgesagt*, *Polymatter*, *Wendover Productions*, *Economics Explained*.
* **Atmosphere**: Welcoming, intellectually stimulating, crystalline clarity, structured world-building.

### Visual & Grading Tokens
* **Base 60% Ground**: Deep Saturated Navy (`#0C1427`) or Crisp Polar White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Secondary 30% Structure**: Slate Neutral (`#334155`) or Mid-Tone Indigo (`#312E81`).
* **Accent 10% Pop**: Solar Tangerine (`#F59E0B`), Coral Red (`#EF4444`), or Electric Mint (`#10B981`).
* **Lighting / Grade**: Flat 2D vector lighting with subtle smooth radial gradients for ambient depth.

### Typography Stack
* **Headline / Hero Title**: *Outfit* (Bold/ExtraBold) or *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Bold, tracking -0.02em).
* **Body / Secondary**: *Cabinet Grotesk* or *General Sans* (Medium, 16px).
* **Numeric Infographics**: *Space Grotesk* (Bold, tabular lining figures).

### Motion & B-Roll Cadence
* Snappy easing curves (Cubic Bezier `0.16, 1, 0.3, 1` spring dynamics).
* Geometric icon metamorphoses (e.g. circle evolves into coin, then into globe).
* Unified illustration universe: All characters, objects, and props share exact stroke weight and corner radii.
