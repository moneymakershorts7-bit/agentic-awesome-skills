# YouTube Marketing & Positioning Engine (`yt-marketing-positioning`)

A systematic persuasion, hook synthesis, and viral positioning skill for faceless YouTube creators. When there is no on-camera creator face, the architecture of the idea itself must perform 100% of the persuasion and social transmission.

---

## 1. Core Mental Models

1. **The Social Transmission Vector (Jonah Berger)**: Viewers do not share videos to help the channel; they share videos as a badge of self-presentation. Content must confer *Social Currency*.
2. **The New Opportunity vs. Improvement Offer (Russell Brunson)**: Improvement offers ("how to work harder at what you failed at") induce fatigue and low CTR. New Opportunities ("the vehicle you were using was structurally broken; here is the new paradigm") spark explosive curiosity and hope.
3. **The Minimum Viable Audience & Category Design (Seth Godin)**: Do not compete with incumbents on generic topics. Define an orthogonal category axis ("History told through forensic accounting", "Coding tutorials for developers who hate bloated frameworks").
4. **The SUCCESs Stickiness Model (Heath & Heath)**: Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories.
5. **The 7 Weapons of Non-Face Authority (Robert Cialdini)**: Establish instant credibility through primary source artifacts (declassified memos, patent schematics, audio recordings, source code diffs).

---

## 2. The Viral Angle Scoring Matrix (VASM)

Every video concept must be evaluated across 5 criteria (0–20 points each, 100 max). **Threshold: ≥75 to greenlight.**

1. **Curiosity Gap Strength (0–20)**: Does the premise create an agonizing cognitive itch that cannot be answered by common sense?
2. **Emotional Arousal Valence (0–20)**: Does it trigger high-arousal emotions (Awe, Anxiety, Outrage, Exhilaration)? Low-arousal content (sadness, contentment) dies in the algorithm.
3. **Social Currency & Identity Badge (0–20)**: Does sharing this video make the viewer look like an insightful insider?
4. **Tangible Visual Evidence Assets (0–20)**: Do we possess primary source artifacts, documents, or real-world data to prove this facelessly?
5. **Minimum Viable Audience Resonance (0–20)**: Does this video speak directly to a passionate tribe with shared grievances and vocabulary?

---

## 3. The 5-Second Hook Formulas

* **Formula 1 (Paradox / Schema Break)**:
  `[Accepted Truth] + [Violent Contradiction by Credible Data] + [Hidden Mechanism]`
  *"Everyone thinks index funds are the safest place for their money. But last month, a leaked Federal Reserve audit revealed a liquidity trap that could freeze withdrawals during the next flash crash."*
* **Formula 2 (High-Stakes Consequence / Cold Open)**:
  `[Specific Number/Loss] + [Trivial Action] + [The Impending Realization]`
  *"In 1998, a single missing hyphen in a line of code vaporized a $135 million spacecraft in 4 minutes. But what investigators found in the autopsy terrified NASA far more than the explosion."*
* **Formula 3 (Insider Confession / Declassified File)**:
  `[Insider Entity] + [Forbidden Reality] + [The Immediate Threat]`
  *"Behind closed doors, semiconductor executives will never admit this. But buried on page 412 of their latest filing is the reason why Taiwan's grid could paralyze global AI in 72 hours."*

---

## 4. The 8-Part Epiphany Bridge Script (Russell Brunson)

1. **Backstory**: Relatable baseline reality before disruption.
2. **The Desire**: External goal vs. internal motive.
3. **The Wall**: The catastrophic obstacle stopping traditional solutions.
4. **The Epiphany**: The counter-intuitive discovery of the hidden mechanism.
5. **The Plan**: Strategic blueprint designed to exploit the mechanism.
6. **The Conflict**: Friction against established incumbents or systems.
7. **The Climax**: Objective proof of victory or tragic collapse.
8. **The Transformation**: Universal takeaway granted to the viewer.
