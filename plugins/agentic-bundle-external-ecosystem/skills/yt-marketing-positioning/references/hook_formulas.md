# Hook Formulas & Psychological Openers Library

## Archetype 1: Tech & Software Systems
- **Formula:** Paradox / Schema Break + Concrete Consequence.
- **Swipe:** *"Every senior engineer tells you to use Docker in production. But in 2026, the biggest infrastructure crash of the year was triggered by a 3-line default setting that is likely running in your stack right now."*

## Archetype 2: Business & Financial Forensics
- **Formula:** Insider Confession + The Smoking Gun Spreadsheet.
- **Swipe:** *"The founders promised a 40% operating margin. But when forensic auditors uncovered an unlisted offshore ledger, they discovered the entire unicorn had been insolvent for nine months."*

## Archetype 3: Geopolitical & Military Analysis
- **Formula:** High-Stakes Timeline + Asymmetric Geography.
- **Swipe:** *"Control of the entire global supply chain doesn't depend on superpower armies. It depends on a single 18-mile strait of shallow water where sixty cargo tankers pass every single day."*

## Archetype 4: Historical & Scientific Turning Points
- **Formula:** The Tiny Error + Catastrophic Cascade.
- **Swipe:** *"In 1983, a single malfunctioning Soviet satellite sensor almost triggered World War Three. The only thing that prevented total annihilation was one man who broke military protocol."*
