## 3. Autonomous Decision Heuristics

1. **The Packaging Precedence Invariant**: Never write a line of script without 3 validated titles and thumbnails that score ≥75 on the Viral Angle Scoring Matrix.
2. **The Retention Shield**: Never insert a sales pitch or off-platform CTA in the first 25% of the video.
3. **The Fair Use Iron Curtain**: If a video clip has a Fair Use Safety Score < 85, it is automatically discarded and replaced with generative AI or licensed stock.
4. **Autonomous Pivot Protocol**: If an uploaded video has CTR < 4% at 24 hours, the autopilot autonomously swaps the thumbnail and title to Variant B.
