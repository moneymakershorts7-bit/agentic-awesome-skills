# YouTube Thumbnail Psychology Engine (`yt-thumbnail-psychology`)

A visual cognitive engineering skill for high-speed mobile glanceability. Viewers do not read mobile feeds; they satisfice at 120–160 items per minute. A successful thumbnail must achieve comprehension and curiosity in under 500 milliseconds.

---

## 1. Core Mental Models

1. **The Structural Skeleton (Rudolf Arnheim)**: The 16:9 canvas contains an invisible field of forces. Elements off-axis create directional visual tension. Centripetal balance focuses inward (forensic mystery); centrifugal balance pushes outward (catastrophe/growth).
2. **The 3-Element Packaging Rule**:
   $$\text{Packaging Unit} = \text{Element 1 (Focal Subject)} + \text{Element 2 (Context Anchor)} + \text{Element 3 (Curiosity Trigger)}$$
   A 4th element doubles cognitive load and halves mobile glanceability.
3. **Figure-Ground Stratification (Ellen Lupton)**: Depth layering into 3 distinct z-index planes (Foreground subject with >3:1 luminance contrast, Midground context, and Darkened/blurred background).
4. **Cognitive Friction Elimination (Steve Krug)**: 60-MPH Billboard rule. Eliminate visual clutter. Zero text duplication between title and thumbnail.
5. **The Timestamp Dead Zone**: The bottom-right corner (~20% width × 25% height) is covered by YouTube's time badge (`12:45`). Never place faces, critical data, or text there.

---

## 2. Title-Thumbnail Interplay Matrix (The Complementarity Law)

The title and thumbnail operate as an entangled narrative binary:
$$\text{Click Propensity} = \text{Setup (Title)} \times \text{Payoff / Stakes (Thumbnail)}$$

| Strategy | Title Role (Intellectual Setup) | Thumbnail Role (Visceral Payoff) | Example Pair |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unsolved Mystery** | States unresolved paradox. | Shows forbidden artifact / anomaly. | **Title:** *Why The FBI Quietly Seized This Remote Mine*<br>**Thumb:** Desert satellite view + Red Box + *"CLASSIFIED"* |
| **Catastrophe** | States economic / physical tipping point. | Shows exact point of fracture. | **Title:** *The 72-Hour Flaw That Could Collapse The Grid*<br>**Thumb:** Glowing severed high-voltage line + map blackout |
| **Asymmetric Edge** | Teases an unfair advantage. | Shows undeniable mechanical contrast. | **Title:** *How Wall Street Elites Trade Before News Breaks*<br>**Thumb:** Retail broker feed vs Supercomputer direct tap |

---

## 3. Typography & Color Specifications

* **Word Count**: Strictly **2 to 4 words maximum**.
* **Fonts**: Ultra-bold geometric or grotesque sans-serif (*Impact, Anton, Montserrat Black, Bebas Neue*).
* **Color Hierarchy**:
  * Base Background (60–70%): Deep Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) or Matte Charcoal (`#18181B`).
  * Contrast Anchor (20–25%): Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  * Emotional Pop (10–15%): Hazard Yellow (`#FFD600`), Crimson Red (`#DC2626`), or Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`).
* **Luminance Contrast**: Text must exceed **4.5:1** contrast ratio against its backing.

---

## 4. Pre-Flight Thumbnail Review Scorecard (10-Point Gate)

1. [ ] **3-Element Rule**: Exactly 1 Subject + 1 Context Anchor + 1 Curiosity Element.
2. [ ] **160×90px Mobile Squint Test**: Readable and instantly identifiable in under 1 second.
3. [ ] **Luminance Contrast**: Subject edges have >3:1 contrast against backdrop.
4. [ ] **Timestamp Safe Zone**: Bottom-right corner completely clear.
5. [ ] **Zero Text Duplication**: No identical words between title and thumbnail.
6. [ ] **Micro-Copy Limit**: Text strictly ≤ 4 words.
7. [ ] **Typography**: Ultra-bold, clean sans-serif with subtle drop-shadow or backing plate.
8. [ ] **Arnheim Visual Weight**: Dominant subject anchored on left two-thirds.
9. [ ] **Curiosity Gap**: Presents an anomaly or stakes, never the final answer.
10. [ ] **First 45s Sync**: Visual promise validated in the first 45 seconds of the script.
