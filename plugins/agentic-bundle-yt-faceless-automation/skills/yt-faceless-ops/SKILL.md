# YouTube Faceless Channel Operations Engine (`yt-faceless-ops`)

An industrial operations, legal shield, and autonomous multi-channel execution system for faceless YouTube media production networks.

---

## 1. Core Mental Models

1. **Modular Production Subsystems (Sam Carpenter)**: Production is broken down into autonomous functional nodes with strict input/output schemas (Ideation, Packaging, Scripting, Audio, Visuals, Polish, QA).
2. **Lean Hypothesis Validation (Eric Ries)**: A video is an empirical experiment. Test topic and hook hypotheses with small batch sizes; measure CTR and 30s retention; execute packaging or pacing pivots based on data.
3. **The 4-Factor Fair Use Fortress (17 U.S.C. § 107)**: Fair use is not an afterthought; it is an engineered production constraint. Third-party media must be transformed via continuous critical commentary, visual framing, and duration caps (3–6s max).
4. **Portfolio Risk Diversification**: Run independent niche channels sharing centralized toolchains and asset libraries while keeping AdSense, branding, and platform accounts decoupled.

---

## 2. Gawande's 5 Production Pause-Points

* **Pause-Point Alpha (Packaging Gate)**: Validates title curiosity gap, 160×90px mobile thumbnail test, and zero text duplication before scripting begins.
* **Pause-Point Bravo (Script Gate)**: Validates sub-15s hook, 145 WPM cadence, visual direction cues, and Flesch-Kincaid Grade 6–8 readability before audio synthesis.
* **Pause-Point Charlie (Audio Gate)**: Validates -14.0 LUFS integrated loudness, -1.0 dBTP ceiling, zero TTS pronunciation errors, and breath pacing.
* **Pause-Point Delta (Edit & Fair Use Gate)**: Validates visual cut frequency (every 3–5s), audio ducking (-18 dB on BGM), and Section 107 Fair Use Safety Score ≥ 85.
* **Pause-Point Echo (Pre-Flight Gate)**: Validates 12-hour unlisted YouTube copyright clearance, thumbnail mobile contrast, chapters/timestamps, and AI synthetic content disclosures.

## 3. Fair Use Safety Score (FSS) Calculator

$$\text{FSS} = S_1 (\text{Purpose: 0-25}) + S_2 (\text{Duration: 0-25}) + S_3 (\text{Treatment: 0-25}) + S_4 (\text{Audio Masking: 0-25})$$

* **Score < 60**: PROHIBITED. High strike risk. Replace with licensed stock or generative AI.
* **Score 60–84**: BORDERLINE. Reframe, shorten, and add analytical overlays.
* **Score 85–100**: APPROVED. Fortress-grade transformative fair use.
