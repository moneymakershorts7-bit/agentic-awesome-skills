# Anti-Slop Visual Taste Checklist (Pre-Flight Gate)

A 12-point DO-CONFIRM verification checklist to eliminate amateur "AI slop" from YouTube thumbnails, B-roll sequences, and visual packages.

---

## The 12-Point Gate

1. **[ ] Archetype Purity**: Is the entire video package (thumbnail, title card, B-roll, lower-thirds) anchored to exactly ONE aesthetic archetype (e.g., Documentary Noir, Swiss Editorial, Technical Systems)?
2. **[ ] Negative Prompt Sanitization**: Have all AI generation prompts been sanitized to ban waxy skin, purple/cyan neon haze, floating 3D coins, ungrounded gloss, and cartoonish expressions?
3. **[ ] 60-30-10 Color Balance**: Does the frame adhere to 60% neutral ground, 30% structural secondary, and 10% emotional accent? No rainbow gradients or competing saturated hues.
4. **[ ] Luminance Contrast Verification**: Does the primary subject or title text achieve at least $4.5:1$ contrast against its immediate backing (WCAG AA)?
5. **[ ] 160×90px Mobile Squint Test**: Downscaled to 160×90px, is the silhouette of the subject and message immediately recognizable within 500 milliseconds?
6. **[ ] Timestamp Dead Zone Clearance**: Is the bottom-right corner (~20% width × 25% height) completely free of faces, key evidence, or critical text?
7. **[ ] Typographic Discipline**: Are there at most TWO font families used? Is the headline tracking tightened (-0.02em to -0.04em) and stamp tracking open (+0.12em)?
8. **[ ] Text Brevity**: Is thumbnail text strictly 2 to 4 words maximum? Is there zero text duplication with the YouTube title?
9. **[ ] Organic Material Grounding**: Do visual assets look physically grounded with authentic texture (paper grain, 35mm film grain, matte slate, archival print)?
10. **[ ] Eradication of Chartjunk**: Are all graphs and charts stripped of gratuitous 3D extrusion, decorative reflections, and wireframe globes (Tufte compliance)?
11. **[ ] Emotional Tone Calibration**: Does the facial expression and visual weight accurately match the gravity of the subject (no hysterical open-mouth party faces on serious geopolitical or financial topics)?
12. **[ ] First 45-Second Continuity**: Does the first visual beat in the video immediately match the color palette and promise established by the thumbnail?

---

## Scoring & Action Rules

- **12/12 Checked**: `PASS` — Greenlit for production and publication.
- **10–11 Checked**: `WARN` — Polish required on failing elements prior to final render.
- **<10 Checked**: `REJECT` — Major aesthetic defect. Re-run TasteEngine prompt cleanser and color calibration.
