# The 10 Psychological Hook Blueprints & VPR-100 Rubric

This reference specifies the 10 production-tested hook architectures and the mathematical Viral Potential Rubric (VPR-100).

## 1. The 10 Psychological Hook Blueprints

### Blueprint 1: The Paradoxical Anomaly (Berger Awe + Loewenstein Anomaly)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Visual depicts an apparent impossibility or counter-intuitive event.
- **Verbal Script**: *"The biggest mistake every senior developer makes is writing clean code on day one."*
- **Mechanism**: System 1 prediction error triggers Loewenstein epistemic drive to resolve the paradox.

### Blueprint 2: The Catastrophic Loss Warning (Kahneman Loss Aversion + Cialdini Scarcity)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: High-contrast warning graphic or system red alert.
- **Verbal Script**: *"If your database does this one thing, you’re losing 30% of your revenue right now without knowing it."*
- **Mechanism**: Kahneman loss aversion ($\lambda = 2.25$) activates the amygdala and prevents thumb-skips.

### Blueprint 3: The Hostage Narrative Open Loop (Zeigarnik + Loewenstein Information Gap)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Jump cut to the peak climax of an experiment or incident.
- **Verbal Script**: *"In 30 seconds, I’ll show you why this \$10 hack destroyed a \$10,000 server... but first, look at this wire."*
- **Mechanism**: Establishes an uncompleted cognitive loop while opening a sub-loop, maintaining retention past 0:15.

### Blueprint 4: The In-Group Social Currency Gate (Berger Social Currency + Cialdini Unity)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Distinctive insider artifact or terminal session.
- **Verbal Script**: *"This is the one CLI tool that 99% of junior devs don't know exists, but Staff engineers use daily."*
- **Mechanism**: Status acquisition and in-group tribal identity.

### Blueprint 5: The Contrast Shock (Cialdini Contrast + Kahneman Fluency)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:01**: Chaotic, broken, low-contrast state A.
- **Timecode 0:01–0:03**: Instant snap cut to a pristine, flawless state B.
- **Verbal Script**: *"This took 6 hours. This took 6 seconds. Here is the exact pipeline."*
- **Mechanism**: Retinal contrast reset and immediate cognitive ease appeal.

### Blueprint 6: The Moral Outrage Mobilizer (Berger High-Arousal Negative Emotion)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Shocking statistical evidence or corporate policy excerpt.
- **Verbal Script**: *"Big tech companies have been secretly charging you for idle bandwidth, and here is the proof."*
- **Mechanism**: Mobilizing sympathetic autonomic arousal ($\beta > 0$) driving comment velocity and shares.

### Blueprint 7: The Asymmetric Secret Reveal (Loewenstein Asymmetry + Schultz RPE)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Intimate close-up, confidential tone.
- **Verbal Script**: *"I wasn't supposed to show you this backend configuration, but they just patched the loophole."*
- **Mechanism**: Scarcity + dopamine surge on anticipated illicit knowledge.

### Blueprint 8: The Micro-Commitment Challenge (Cialdini Consistency + Fogg Brain Cycles)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Rapid 2-option visual challenge.
- **Verbal Script**: *"Pick A or B in the next 2 seconds. If you chose A, your security is already compromised."*
- **Mechanism**: Immediate behavioral compliance (Action) loads the investment cycle.

### Blueprint 9: The Cognitive Disruption / Pattern Break (Kahneman System 1 Reset)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:02**: Unexpected physical action (dropping an object, camera rotation, 0.8s dead silence).
- **Verbal Script**: *[0.8s silence, visual drop]* *"Stop scrolling. Everything you were taught about auth is wrong."*
- **Mechanism**: Thalamocortical sensory reset, forcing conscious System 2 attention.

### Blueprint 10: The Rule of 100 Altruistic Utility (Berger Practical Value + Fogg Action)
- **Timecode 0:00–0:03**: Concrete step-by-step numbered graphic.
- **Verbal Script**: *"Here is a 3-command script that saves \$400 every month on AWS billings. Bookmark this now."*
- **Mechanism**: High perceived ROI; drives "Save" and "Share with team" actions.

### Evaluation Decision Gates:
- **90–100**: Unconditional viral candidate; maximum distribution probability.
- **75–89**: High potential; minor adjustments to hook contrast or open loops.
- **60–74**: Average / churn risk; prune dead air and amplify loss aversion.
- **$<60$**: Immediate rejection. Rewrite required.
