# Video Editing Grammar & Montage Theory 🎬✂️

The comprehensive manual on transition logic, rhythmic cutting, dialogue pacing, and montage construction.

---

## 1. The Core Taxonomy of Cuts

| Cut Type | Technical Definition | Narrative Function | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard Cut** | Immediate switch from Shot A to Shot B | Standard scene progression, real-time dialogue | Standard conversation |
| **J-Cut** | Incoming audio starts before incoming video | Builds anticipation, prepares brain for visual shift | Hear siren before seeing police car |
| **L-Cut** | Outgoing audio continues past incoming video cut | Shows reaction, subtext, emotional aftermath | Character speaks while showing listener's face |
| **Match Cut** | Cut between two objects with matching shape/vector | Metaphorical bridge across space or time | Bone tossed in air $\to$ orbiting spacecraft (*2001: A Space Odyssey*) |
| **Action Cut (Cutting on Action)** | Cut occurs in the middle of a physical movement | Masks the cut entirely, making it feel invisible | Punch thrown in Wide $\to$ lands in Close-Up |
| **Jump Cut** | Cut between two frames of same subject from same angle | Compresses time, shows passage of hours/frustration | Packing a suitcase in frantic micro-jumps |
| **Smash Cut** | Violent shift in tempo, volume, or atmosphere | Comic shock, sudden horror, wake-up call | Screaming nightmare $\to$ serene silent bedroom |
| **Cross-Cutting (Parallel Editing)** | Interweaving two scenes occurring simultaneously | Builds exponential suspense, thematic comparison | The Godfather baptism sequence |

## 2. Dialogue Pacing & Eye-Trace

1. **Never Cut on the Word:**
   - Amateurs cut immediately when a character stops speaking.
   - Masters let the shot linger for 0.5s–1.2s to capture the character's post-speech breath, hesitation, eye shift, or swallow.
2. **The Reaction Shot is Stronger than the Speech:**
   - Often, the most powerful visual information is not the person delivering the bad news, but the silent person receiving it.
3. **Eye-Trace Guiding:**
   - If the focal point of Shot A is in the upper-left third, Shot B's immediate focal point should align nearby, or the viewer spends 300ms hunting for the subject, losing cognitive momentum.

---

## 3. Montage Construction (The Kuleshov Effect & Soviet Montage)

- **Metric Montage:** Cuts based purely on mathematical rhythm/music tempo regardless of content.
- **Rhythmic Montage:** Cuts that match physical tempo within the frame (walking speed, heartbeat, cars passing).
- **Tonal Montage:** Cuts governed by emotional atmosphere, light qualities, and textures.
- **Intellectual / Associative Montage:** Juxtaposing two unrelated ideas to create a third concept (e.g., politician speaking $\to$ herd of sheep).
