# Creative Resources: Filmmaking, 3D & Digital Media Asset Index

## Overview

A curated index of production assets and tools for creators, designers, video editors, and 3D artists. Covers free and open-access royalty-free sound effects, ambient audio, PBR textures, HDRI environments, typography, color palettes, and pre-production templates.

## When to Use
- Sourcing high-quality free assets for video, 3D (Blender/Three.js), motion design, or game dev projects.
- Selecting royalty-free background music, foley sound effects, or ambient soundscapes.
- Discovering open-source software alternatives for editing, rendering, and compositing (DaVinci Resolve, Blender, Krita, Natron).
- Downloading production templates: storyboards, call sheets, budgeting sheets, treatment decks.

## Category Quick Index

### 1. Audio & Sound FX
- **Foley & Sound FX**: Freesound.org, Sonniss GDC Audio Archive, BBC Sound Effects Library.
- **Royalty-Free Music**: Free Music Archive (FMA), Incompetech, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat.

### 2. 3D Assets & Textures
- **PBR Textures & HDRIs**: Poly Haven, AmbientCG, 3Dassets.one.
- **3D Models**: Sketchfab (CC0/CC-BY), Clara.io, CGTrader Free.

### 3. Visuals & Color Grading
- **Color Palettes & LUTs**: Adobe Color, Coolors.co, Freshman LUTs.
- **Stock Footage & Photos**: Pexels, Unsplash, Coverr.co, Mixkit.

### 4. Typography & Icons
- **Type**: Google Fonts, Font Squirrel, Velvetyne Type Foundry.
- **Icons & Vectors**: Lucide Icons, Simple Icons, SVGL, VectorLogoZone.
