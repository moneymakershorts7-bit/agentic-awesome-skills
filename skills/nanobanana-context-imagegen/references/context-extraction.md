# Context Extraction Guide

> Methodologies for analyzing narrative chapters, commentaries, and technical documents to derive spectacular, grounded visual concepts.

---

## 1. The Context Extraction Framework

When an agent is asked to generate images for a manuscript, book chapter, or document, it must follow an analytical sequence rather than jumping straight to prompt drafting:

```
[ Active Document Excerpt ]
            │
            ├─► 1. World & Era Analysis (Architecture, era, materials, geography)
            ├─► 2. Focal Entity Identification (Key characters, symbolic entities, items)
            ├─► 3. Narrative Dramatic Arc (Tension point, climax, contemplation, revelation)
            ├─► 4. Spatial Geometry & Role (Cover, header, full-page illustration, spot art)
            └─► 5. Ambient Atmosphere (Color mood, lighting conditions, weather, emotional weight)
```

---

## 2. Extraction Taxonomy by Document Domain

### A. Ancient & Biblical Narratives (e.g., Book of Daniel)
- **World & Era:** Neo-Babylonian / Achaemenid Persian Empire. Mudbrick architecture, glazed lapis lazuli tiles, monumental winged bulls (Lamassu), Euphrates/Tigris riverbanks, date palms, reed thickets.
- **Costumes & Materials:** Layered linen robes, fringed tunics, bronze jewelry, cylinder seals, leather sandals, ceremonial headdresses.
- **Lighting:** Golden Mesopotamian sunlight, torchlight casting high-contrast shadows on textured relief walls, celestial luminescence (lightning, beryl crystal glow).
- **Tone:** Epic, sacred, reverent, solemn, prophetic.

### B. Literary Fiction & Narrative Non-Fiction
- **Focal Entity:** Identify the central point of interest in the scene. Avoid crowding the image with secondary characters unless a crowd scene is explicitly needed.
- **Visual Tension:** Choose the exact moment of highest dramatic resonance (the decision, the revelation, the aftermath).
- **Environmental Grounding:** Anchor characters with tangible surface textures (weathered timber, damp cobblestones, rain-slick asphalt).

### C. Technical & Academic Texts
- **Metaphorical Translation:** Convert abstract concepts (networks, security, consensus, neural models) into elegant, tangible visual metaphors (e.g. geometric crystalline matrices, optical fiber conduits, architectural interlocking blocks).
- **Compositional Cleanliness:** High contrast, deliberate negative space, restrained color palette (1 dominant, 1 accent).

## 3. Asset Role Mapping

| Asset Type | Target Aspect Ratio | Recommended Framing | Purpose |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Book Cover** | `2:3` or `3:4` | Vertical, strong central focal point, top/bottom negative space for title & author. | Commercial impact, genre signaling. |
| **Chapter Frontispiece** | `3:4` or `4:3` | Full-page framed composition with rich textural detail. | Chapter opening immersion. |
| **Section Banner** | `16:9` or `21:9` | Wide panoramic horizontal landscape or environmental establishing shot. | Visual pacing and section transition. |
| **Character Portrait** | `1:1` or `3:4` | Bust or three-quarter view, shallow depth of field, dramatic sidelight. | Entity representation and likeness. |
| **Spot Illustration** | `1:1` | Isolated iconic subject with soft vignette or neutral backdrop. | Text accent and symbolic pause. |
