# Cinematography, Lighting & Color Aesthetics 💡🎨

The director's manual for crafting visual atmosphere, light ratios, color palettes, and cinematic textures.

---

## 1. Classical Lighting Setups & Directorial Emotion

```
             [Back / Rim Light]
                    │
                    ▼
               [ Subject ]
                 ↗     ↖
                ╱       ╲
   [Key Light] ╱         ╲ [Fill Light]
   (Dominant)             (Shadow softness)
```

1. **Three-Point Lighting (Standard Hollywood):**
   - *Key Light (45° angle):* Primary illumination defining shape and texture.
   - *Fill Light (opposite Key):* Controls shadow depth and contrast ratio.
   - *Rim / Hair Light (behind subject):* Separates subject from dark background.

2. **Chiaroscuro / High-Contrast Low-Key (Film Noir / Thriller / Mystery):**
   - Key light only, fill light set to near zero.
   - Deep rich shadows, mystery, moral ambiguity, psychological conflict.

3. **Rembrandt Lighting (Painterly / Dramatic):**
   - Key light creates a distinctive small triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek.
   - Classic, timeless, deeply dramatic.

4. **Natural Diffuse / High-Key (Uplifting / Clean / Nordic):**
   - Soft, even, wraparound lighting through large diffusion silks.
   - Welcoming, transparent, honest, contemporary tech or lifestyle.

5. **Neon Noir / Cyberpunk / Stylized:**
   - Saturated practical sources (cyan neon, magenta backlight, amber sodium street lamps).
   - High reflectivity on wet streets, glass, or metallic surfaces.

## 2. Color Palettes & Emotional Psychology

| Palette Scheme | Dominant Hues | Psychological Association | Genre Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teal & Orange** | Cyan (#008B8B) + Amber (#FF8C00) | Maximum skin-tone separation, blockbuster action, tension | Transformers, Mad Max, Blade Runner 2049 |
| **Monochromatic Noir** | Charcoal, Platinum, Deep Black | Fatalism, forensic truth, timeless elegance | Mank, Roma, Sin City |
| **Warm Nostalgia** | Golden ochre, tobacco brown, creamy whites | Memory, vintage warmth, historical reflection | The Godfather, Grand Budapest Hotel |
| **Cold Clinical Blue** | Slate grey, ice blue, fluorescent green | Alienation, corporate dread, medical precision | The Social Network, Chernobyl, Ex Machina |
| **Hyper-Vibrant Pop** | Magenta, cobalt blue, electric yellow | Sensory overload, modern youth, viral hook | Euphoria, Spider-Verse |

## 3. Aspect Ratios & Framing Canvas

- **2.39:1 (Cinemascope / Anamorphic):** Widescreen epic grandeur, expansive horizons, architectural framing.
- **16:9 (1.78:1 Standard Video / YouTube):** Modern universal broadcast, documentary essays, computer display native.
- **9:16 (0.56:1 Vertical Mobile / Shorts / Reels):** Intimate portraiture, vertical architecture, fast mobile scanning.
- **4:3 (1.33:1 Academy Ratio):** Retro archival, psychological claustrophobia, historical intimacy.

## 4. Texture, Film Grain & Sensor Emulation

- **35mm Kodak Vision3 5219 (500T):** Organic fine silver halide grain, gentle highlight roll-off, warm skin halation.
- **16mm Grain (Indie / Gritty):** Pronounced kinetic grain texture, vintage documentary realism.
- **Digital Clean Arri Alexa Log-C:** Smooth gradients, ultra-wide 14-stop dynamic range, pristine modern cinema.
