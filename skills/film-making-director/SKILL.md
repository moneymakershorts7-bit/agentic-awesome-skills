# Film Making Director 🎬🎥

The **Film Making Director** is an elite creative and cinematic directing skill. It bridges high-level artistic vision, narrative psychology, documentary authenticity, and technical camera grammar into actionable production blueprints, shot lists, and model-ready visual generation directives.

---

## When to Use This Skill

- Use when conceptualizing and directing any cinematic sequence, documentary, YouTube essay, or commercial.
- Use when designing camera choreography, lens selection, lighting setups, or scene blocking.
- Use when translating story scripts into structured, timecoded shot lists (`SHOT_LIST.md`).
- Use when crafting high-precision visual prompts for AI video generators (Kling, Runway Gen-3, Wan, LTX, Sora).

---

## 🎯 Directorial Core Principles

1. **Every Frame Has a Subtext:** Never shoot passive filler. Every camera angle, lens choice, lighting contrast, and subject movement must reinforce the emotional beat or intellectual thesis.
2. **Grammar of the Lens:**
   - **Wide (16mm - 24mm):** Establishes space, scale, isolation, or overwhelming environment.
   - **Normal (35mm - 50mm):** Human perspective, intimate dialogue, grounded reality.
   - **Telephoto (85mm - 200mm):** Compressed depth of field, intense psychological isolation, macro detail, cinematic bokeh.
   - **Anamorphic / 2.39:1:** Cinematic epic scope, horizontal flares, oval bokeh.
3. **Camera Movement is Motivation:** The camera never moves without psychological impetus:
   - *Dolly In / Push:* Intensifies focus, realization, tension, or impending danger.
   - *Dolly Out / Pull:* Reveals context, isolation, aftermath, or insignificance.
   - *Tracking / Orbit:* Energy, dynamic momentum, entrapment, or shifting perspective.
   - *Handheld / Verité:* Immediate urgency, chaos, documentary realism, or vulnerable honesty.
   - *Static Locked-off:* Cold calculation, suspense, observational truth, or tableau majesty.
4. **Light Shapes Truth:** Direction of key light, shadow depth (chiaroscuro), color temperature (warm tungsten vs cold teal), and atmospheric haze define the mood before a word is spoken.
5. **Directing for AI Video Models:** Translate abstract feelings into precise physical parameters (subject action, camera rig move, lens type, lighting ratio, frame composition).

## 📐 The 5-Phase Directorial Workflow

```
[Phase 1: Vision & Beat Sheet] 
   └── Define thesis, emotional arc, tone, and pacing rhythm
[Phase 2: Visual Bible & Motifs]
   └── Lock character appearances, color palette, lighting scheme, location rules
[Phase 3: Scene Blocking & Camera Choreography]
   └── Design spatial relationship, subject vectors, camera rigs, and lens choices
[Phase 4: Shot List & Visual Scripting]
   └── Time-coded shot table with shot type, lens, angle, action, lighting, and sound cue
[Phase 5: Generation / Production Directives]
   └── Exact model-ready prompts, camera parameters, and director notes for editing
```
